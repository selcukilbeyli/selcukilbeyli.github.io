Private Local Drafts

Write and save notes here without publishing them. Your drafts stay in this browser profile only.

Important: Local drafts are not uploaded or synced. They can be lost if your browser data is cleared or the device is lost. Export backups regularly. Do not use a shared computer for sensitive material.

When ready to publish, open the Pages CMS editor, create a Quick Note, and paste your text. Files can be uploaded in the publishing editor.
